1. Connect Deriv

DEMO first. The scanner does not assume a market supports Rise/Fall: it discovers active symbols and verifies the actual CALL/PUT contracts returned by Deriv before enabling a trade.

2. Trading & Martingale controls

Martingale is always ON in this build. A loss increases the stake for the next trade only. Any winning trade immediately resets the next stake to the Initial stake. The maximum stake and loss-streak limit are hard safety caps.
