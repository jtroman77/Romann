Roman Single / Roman Double

Choose Step 100, 200, or 300. Roman Single runs one market. Roman Double runs two selected markets at the same time, each with its own independent single-tick signal and Martingale chain.

1. Mode

Roman SinglePick one Step market and run it independently.
Roman DoublePick two different Step markets and run both independently.

2. Account

REAL mode can place trades against your real Deriv Options balance. START remains disabled until every selected Step market is verified for CALL/PUT on the selected account.

3. Strategy

Signal logic is the same as your current bot: one rising tick → RISE; one falling tick → FALL. Win resets that market's stake to the initial stake. Loss multiplies only that market's stake by the Martingale multiplier. In Roman Double, Market A and Market B do not share a recovery chain.
