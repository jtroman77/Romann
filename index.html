Romanx Bull + Bear Live Tick Bot

Current Deriv public Options WebSocket + private demo trading connection. One tick confirms direction; trade duration is editable. FIX v5.

1. Connect Deriv

Market prices come from Deriv's public WebSocket. Trading uses your authenticated demo WebSocket. This avoids the subscription errors from the earlier build.

2. Strategy

1 rising tick → RISE. 1 falling tick → FALL. That single price movement is the entry confirmation; the contract duration is separately editable from 1–5 ticks (default 1). Win → reset stake and stay on the same market. Loss → Martingale, wait, switch Bull ↔ Bear. The bot stops when Session P/L reaches your Total profit target.
