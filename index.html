Roman Snipe V3 — Allow Equals

One snipe at a time. The bot scans eligible Derived Rise/Fall markets, waits for the strongest stable setup, uses Allow Equals, and turns OFF after the first winning contract. Press START again for the next snipe.

1. Auto market scanner

No Double mode and no manual market selection. The scanner discovers eligible Derived Rise/Fall markets from Deriv, verifies CALL/PUT availability, watches their live ticks, and routes the single trading engine to the strongest stable setup. If no market qualifies, it displays NO STABLE MARKET BY INDICATORS and waits.

2. Account

REAL mode can place trades against your real Deriv Options balance. START remains disabled until at least one scanned market is verified for Allow Equals on the selected account.

3. Strategy

Snipe logic: EMA9/EMA21 plus the stability filters select the market. The first entry uses your editable initial stake and tick duration (default 3). Every trade is Allow Equals. After a LOSS the bot waits, blocks that market for the current recovery chain, and searches again with stricter stability. Before recovery it checks the actual proposal payout and calculates the exact stake needed; if the payout math does not fit your multiplier or max-stake limit, that market is skipped. The first WIN turns the bot OFF until START SNIPE is pressed again.
