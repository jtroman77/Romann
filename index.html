Roman Auto Scanner — Rise/Fall

The bot watches eligible Derived Rise/Fall markets at the same time, ranks stability, trades the strongest qualifying market for 3 ticks, and automatically switches when stability breaks or after a loss.

1. Auto market scanner

No Double mode and no manual market selection. The scanner discovers eligible Derived Rise/Fall markets from Deriv, verifies CALL/PUT availability, watches their live ticks, and routes the single trading engine to the strongest stable setup. If no market qualifies, it displays NO STABLE MARKET BY INDICATORS and waits.

2. Account

REAL mode can place trades against your real Deriv Options balance. START remains disabled until at least one scanned market is verified for CALL/PUT on the selected account.

3. Strategy

Auto-routing logic: EMA9/EMA21 establishes direction; activity-weighted price alignment, tick-speed consistency, tick-intensity consistency, directional efficiency, reversal checks, and overextension checks decide stability. A market must remain qualified across successive live ticks before entry. Each contract is fixed at 3 ticks. After a LOSS, the Martingale stake is preserved but that market is temporarily excluded while the engine switches to another stable market. After a WIN, stake resets. If the active market reverses before a new entry, trading pauses on it and the scanner switches.
