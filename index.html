Romanx Step 100

Step 100 only • 1-tick confirmation • editable 1–5 tick contract • Demo or Real account

1. Account

REAL mode places trades against your real Deriv Options balance. The bot will refuse to start unless Deriv returns a real Options account and Step 100 CALL/PUT contracts for it.
Public Step 100 prices use Deriv's public Options WebSocket. Trading uses the OTP WebSocket URL returned for the selected Demo or Real account.

2. Strategy

Strategy: one rising Step 100 tick → RISE; one falling tick → FALL. That one movement is the confirmation. The contract duration is separate and defaults to 1 tick. Win → reset stake. Loss → Martingale, wait, then read a fresh Step 100 tick before the next entry.
