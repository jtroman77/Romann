2. Strategy

Skew 5 Up: only an UP tick confirms a RISE entry. Skew 5 Down: only a DOWN tick confirms a FALL entry. Win → reset stake and stay on the same market. Loss → Martingale, wait, then switch Up ↔ Down. The rare counter-move can be large, so the maximum-stake stop remains active.
